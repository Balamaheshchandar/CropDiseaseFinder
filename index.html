How Naïve Bayes Works

  1. Step 1: Collect training reviews – Labeled sample reviews (Positive & Negative) form our training dataset.
  2. Step 2: Calculate class probabilities – Compute prior probabilities $P(\text{Positive})$ and $P(\text{Negative})$ based on dataset proportions.
  3. Step 3: Calculate word probabilities – Count word frequencies per class and apply Laplace smoothing:
    P(word | Class) = (Count(word, Class) + 1) / (Total Words in Class + Vocabulary Size)
  4. Step 4: Calculate Positive and Negative scores – For each word in the input, sum log-likelihoods with class priors to prevent arithmetic underflow.
  5. Step 5: Select the class with higher probability – Convert log-scores back to normalized probabilities and assign the class with the maximum value.
